# Methodology
Research question: What can available evidence tell us about public transport ridership patterns and route-level demand in Bareilly?

The application accepts dated passenger-count records. Before analysis, document whether each row represents a route-day, trip, stop or another unit. Required fields are service_date and passengers. Dates and passenger counts are validated; negative counts are rejected; optional fields are retained; duplicate rows are ignored.

Metrics include total imported passengers, daily totals and route totals. Interpret them only when reporting scope and coverage are known and comparable.

General traffic counts are not bus ridership. Modelled demand projections are not observed passenger counts. Imported records are not citywide totals unless the source supports that claim.
